Migration Steps

  1. Replace the FormGroup shape with a plain interface and a signal() holding it.
  2. Swap the new FormGroup construction for form(model, schema).
  3. Move each Validators.x into the schema as required(), minLength(), email().
  4. Turn group-level validators into validate() on the field that shows the error, reading siblings via valueOf().
  5. Replace formControlName="x" with [formField]="myForm.x" and drop [formGroup].
  6. Replace control.hasError('required') with field().errors() or field().getError('required').
  7. Replace markAllAsTouched() plus a validity check with submit(myForm, action).
  8. Delete valueChanges subscriptions and toSignal() bridges: the model signal is the value.
  9. Remove ReactiveFormsModule from the component imports.
